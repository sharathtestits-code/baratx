## 1. Thesis (one line)

**Acquire Gen Z with short-form video about real BaratX fights. Keep the product as sided text debate — not another Reels feed.**

---

## 2. ICP

**Primary:** Indian Gen Z, 18–24, college + early career, Tier-1/Tier-2, English+Hindi comfortable, heavy on IG Reels / YT Shorts / WhatsApp / Telegram.

**Wedges**
- **A — Campus & Culture (70% effort):** Entertainment + Sports + daily Square question → reach
- **B — Builders & Hustle (30%):** Startups “Fund it / Pass” reframed as hustle content → denser users

**Activation definition (locked):** first post with a side/topic — not just signup.

---

## 3. Product gates (before any paid spend)

1. **Entertainment first (live density):** top 3–5 debates each have **≥5 non-official real replies**  
   - Live check 2026-08-09: Entertainment/News/Politics have open debates; **Sports & Startups topics ≈ 0 open debates** — open rooms there before featuring them in Reels.
2. **≥5 user-written daily questions/week** (not only RSS/headline reformats)
3. **3–5 vertical clips ready** from real activity (screen-record sharp replies / Fund it–Pass)

If gate #1 fails → seed with 10–15 friends first. Do not push Reels into empty rooms.

**Day-1 runbook + contacts:** [GENZ-START-NOW.md](./GENZ-START-NOW.md) · [barathx_genz_gtm.md](./barathx_genz_gtm.md)

## 6. Campus Voice program (replaces invented ₹150-only story)

**Offer (bootstrap, honest):**
- Title: **Founding Campus Voice**
- Perks: public shoutout on IG + First 50 list + WhatsApp scout group + early badge when ready
- Optional cash (only if you can pay): ₹300–500/month for reps who host **1 room/week + generate 5 real replies**
- Do **not** promise ₹150 Founding rooms in public until it’s built in-product

**Quota:** 15–25 reps across 3–5 colleges (mix Tier-1 + ≥2 Tier-2).

**Rep weekly job**
1. Post 1 real take on today’s Square question  
2. Co-host or open 1 campus-relevant debate (hostel, placements, fest, cricket)  
3. Share to IG/WhatsApp Story with `barathx.com` link  

---

## 7. Content cadence (after gate #1)

- **Daily:** 15–30s clip of a real exchange → Reels + Shorts + Telegram  
- **Daily:** “today’s question” as native Reels/carousel (signup CTA)  
- **Weekly:** 60s room recap  
- **Weekly:** one Campus Voice spotlight  
- **Tone:** sharp group-chat friend, not startup brochure  

---

## 8. 90-day phases (unchanged targets, corrected inputs)

**Days 1–15 — Close gates, ₹0 ads**
- Seed 10–15 real replies in Entertainment/Sports  
- Recruit 8–10 Campus Voices  
- Publish 5 real clips  
- Target: ≥1 debate/Arena with 5+ real replies; 3+ clips ready  

**Days 16–45 — Organic**
- Ambassadors live weekly  
- Daily Reels/Shorts/Telegram  
- Target: 25+ Voices, 500+ non-official posts, 3+ clips at 10k+ views  

**Days 46–75 — Small paid on winners**
- Boost top 3 organic clips; 5–10 micro shoutouts  
- Target: under-25 DAU ↑; D7 retention ≥25% from campus  

**Days 76–90 — Decide**
- ≥40% new signups under-25 from campus channels  
- ≥3 organic viral clips from real product  
- ≥30% D7 retention Gen Z  
- ≥50 ambassador rooms with 5+ participants  
- Pass → scale colleges + spend; Fail retention → fix product loop first
