# Sales & Growth Lead brief — Founding 100 (surprise pay)

**Canonical public line (bio, outreach, posts, site):**

> 100 Founding spots, earned by opening a debate that gets real engagement, not by signing up.

No number. No rupee sign. Nothing that reads as task-for-payment.

**Private confirmation only** (after status `payable` / `paid` — the person already earned the spot):

> You're in. Small thank-you on the way — ₹150, no strings.

(Paid variant: `You're in. Thank-you sent — ₹150, no strings.`)

---

## Why (non-negotiable)

The second someone sees **₹150** before they've acted, that number becomes the reason to act — and you pay people to farm low-effort "debates." Hide the amount until after the real behavior, and you filter for people who want to argue; the thank-you lands as recognition, not bait. Same ~₹15,000 total either way.

## Rules for Sales & Growth

| Surface | Allowed |
|---------|---------|
| IG/X bio, outreach DMs, Reels captions, landing, Rewards (pre-earn) | Public line only — **no ₹** |
| In-app after `payable` / `paid` | Surprise thank-you with ₹150 |
| Ops `/bx-ops` | Full amounts (internal) |
| Square Race prize ranges | Separate product — keep race framing distinct; do not bolt ₹ onto Founding public pitch |

## Banned phrases (public)

- first 100 get ₹150 / Founding ₹150 / ₹150 for opening a room
- signup bonus / cash for joining / pay-to-post framing
- any rupee amount tied to Founding before `payable`
