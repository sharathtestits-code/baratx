## Slide 9 — How we pay (ops / partners only)
# How we pay — not for public creatives

### Founding 100 — surprise thank-you
- Earn via real debate + engagement  
- Private reveal after `payable`: **₹150**  
- No strings · admin marks paid (UPI)  
- Budget ceiling ≈ **₹15,000** total  
- Hide ₹ until after the behavior  

### Square Race — every 14 days
- Highest-liked Home post wins  
- Min **25** likes to qualify  
- Prize **₹150 – ₹500** (scales)  
- Official / blue likes ignored  
- Keep Race framing separate from Founding public pitch  

**Principle:** reward the behavior we want repeated — real debates, not installs.
