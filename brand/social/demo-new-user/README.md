# New-user demo videos (20–25s each)

Short in-app demos so new users understand **where to post** and **where everyone sees posts**.

## Where posts live (product map)

| Place | What you see | Who sees it |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| **Square** (`/feed`) | Home feed of takes | **Yes — the common place.** **For you / Global** = everyone’s public takes. **Following** = people you follow (+ you). |
| **Arenas** | Topic floors + debate rooms | Arena members / people in that debate |
| **Live** | Live rooms + Live Talk A/V | People in that room |
| **Profile** | Your posts | Anyone who opens your profile |

**Correct:** Square is the main public square where everyone can see each other’s posts (use **For you / Global**). Arenas and Live are focused rooms, not the all-posts feed.
