## The product, straight — no inflation

- **What it is:** Pick a side, argue it live. Sided debates (Agree/Disagree), not a feed you scroll.
- **Positioning line:** *"The group chat where India actually argues."* / *"No Reels feed. Just say it live."*
- **Real structure:** Square (daily question + takes), Live (audio/video sided debate rooms, max 15 people), 6 Arenas (Startups, Sports, Politics, Entertainment, News, Spirituality), Communities (member-run, local/interest groups), a Founding ₹150 incentive for the first 100 people who post a real civic problem or open a debate that gets real engagement.
- **Where it actually stands right now:** Early. Very few real users. Most Arena activity is still official/seed content, not organic. Do not write copy that implies scale or traction that doesn't exist yet — Gen Z will smell it immediately and it torches trust permanently. Every post should be honest about being early, or better, make "small and real" the whole appeal.
- **The one thing to protect:** BarathX's bet is real, live disagreement over algorithmic video. Don't dilute it by chasing Reels-style formats in the *product*. You CAN and SHOULD use short-form video to market it — clips *of* real debates — you just don't need to add a Reels tab to win attention.

## The voice rules

1. **Never post AI-smooth copy.** Contractions, specific opinion, uneven sentence length. No "furthermore," no three-adjective lists, no em-dash-every-sentence rhythm.
2. **One consistent POV, not trend-chasing.** Real disagreement, no performance, no AI slop, small right now and proud of it.
3. **No performative stances.** Opinions only on product-relevant territory (India's public discourse, algorithmic feeds, authenticity).
4. **Never hard-sell.** No "Join now!!", no fake scarcity. Founding 100 is real — say the real number when used.
5. **Banned phrases:** "game-changer," "revolutionize," "unlock," "in today's fast-paced world," "we're thrilled to announce," "seamless," "empower," any sentence starting with "Imagine a world where."

## Post formula

**Announcement → Problem → Solution → How it works (3-4 steps) → soft CTA.**

Hook types (pick **one** per post):
- Pain-point dunk
- Bragworthy stat (real number only, even if small)
- Counter-narrative declaration
- Behind-the-curtain reveal (real screen recording > produced clip)

Soft CTAs that fit us: "comment BX," "reply and I'll send the link," "try it and tell me it's not different."

## What to ask the founder before writing anything new

If there isn't an actual moment (real debate, real number, real screen recording) — **ask before writing**. Don't invent traction or quotes. If nothing real happened this week, write mechanic / positioning instead.
