## Primary post (recommended)

BarathX is in soft launch.

We're building **India's public square** — a place for sided debate and real replies, not another feed you like and leave.

### What we're trying to achieve
India already argues everywhere — WhatsApp groups, comment sections, campus chats. Those takes **disappear**. There's no public record, no clear side, and almost no chance a stranger answers you seriously.

BarathX exists so India can **argue on the record**: pick a side, make the case, get answered by a human.

### What you can do on BarathX today
- **Square** — short posts, real conversation (reply > like)
- **Arenas** — sided debates (Agree / Disagree) on sports, politics, entertainment, news, spirituality, startups
- **Live rooms** — open a room, argue it live, invite others in
- **Live Talk** — voice in the room when the fight needs more than text
- **Communities** — city and interest groups that aren't the same as Arenas
- **Founding 100** — 100 Founding spots, earned by opening a debate that gets real engagement, not by signing up

Human takes only. No AI slop.

If you care how India talks in public — founders, campus, culture, news — leave your first take early.
