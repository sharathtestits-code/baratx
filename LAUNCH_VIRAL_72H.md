# BaratX viral soft launch — 72 hours

**Goal:** density in India (Hyderabad / Bangalore / Delhi NCR), not empty global followers.  
**Success:** 50–150 signups who **post once**, 20+ replies on X launch thread, IG Story views from real people.

Brand: **BaratX** · URL: **https://barathx.com** · English-first · Never claim Hindi/Telugu yet.

---

## Rules that make it viral (do these)

1. **Ask for a reply, not a like** — “Drop your city” / “What’s broken in public discourse?”
2. **Seed before blast** — 20–30 DMs *before* the public post goes up.
3. **Post when India is awake** — 8–10am IST and 7–10pm IST.
4. **Screenshot proof** — landing + feed in every thread/Reel.
5. **Follow-up same day** — reply to every comment in the first 3 hours.

## Do not

- Buy followers / fake engagement  
- Claim “Twitter killer” or multi-language  
- Post once and disappear  
- Blast strangers without a personal ask
